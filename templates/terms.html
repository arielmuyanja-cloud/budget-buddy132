2. Your data — what happens to your CSV

We know a bank or card statement is sensitive, so here's exactly how it's handled:

  • In transit: the connection between your browser and Budget Buddy is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS), same as your bank's website.
  • At rest: if you create an account, your transactions are stored in our database on Render's managed hosting infrastructure, which encrypts stored data. We never see or store your full card or bank account number — that never touches Budget Buddy, since checkout happens with our payment processor.

  • AI features: if you use an AI-powered feature (like Ask AI), a summary of your account's financial figures is sent to our third-party AI provider (OpenAI and/or Google) solely to generate that response.
