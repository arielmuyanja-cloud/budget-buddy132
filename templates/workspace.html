STEP 3 · VERIFY

⚠️ Operational Risk Check

Transaction data cannot answer these questions. The team must verify them.

  1. Does it support a critical, client-facing workflow?
  2. Does the team actively log into it?
  3. Does it store important company/client files?
  4. Does it connect or pass data to other critical systems?

Logic: Yes to Q1 or Q4 → High. Otherwise Yes to Q2 or Q3 → Medium. All No → Low.

Use Risk check beside any staged decision to save the answers.
